## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Finished premium family-friendly game key art for an original school miniapp arcade. Landscape 3:2 composition, no interface mockup. Polished cinematic stylized 3D, tactile materials, convincing gentle lighting, strong clear silhouette readable in a small game card, rich but restrained colors. Subject centered with room around it, slightly dramatic low three-quarter camera. No logos, watermarks, frames or UI.

The first four generations also explicitly requested no text.

## UI scope and validation

Illustrated cards, start screens, the featured garden banner, and consistent dialog chrome. Individual playfields retain their own mechanics and visuals. The case result replaces the reel within a fixed area; action rows retain their height during opening, retries, and reveal. Deferred animation frames cannot overwrite a completed result; repeated openings restore the reel visibility.

## Card typography

At the user’s request, cards show only the illustration, a 28 px title and a 44×44 leaderboard control. Subtitles, reward badges, level counters, progress captions and the case collection label were removed from the catalog. Rewards and progress remain available in their dedicated views.
